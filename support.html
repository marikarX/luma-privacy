Luma support

LUMA SUPPORT - SYNAPSE ONE Contact: info@synapse-one.com

For gameplay or technical issues, include: - Game version (Windows candidate 0.6.2). - Windows version, graphics card and driver version. - Chamber or experiment name and the exact steps leading to the issue. - What you expected and what happened instead. - A screenshot or short recording, if it helps.

Do not send passwords or payment credentials. Review logs or save files before sharing them. We may ask for a relevant log or a copy of your progress to reproduce a problem; back up your saves before trying a workaround.

Extract all ZIP contents together before running Luma.exe. Keep luma_optics.dll beside it. Restarting a chamber does not erase earned progress. Pause includes sound controls; Main Menu Resume retains the current arrangement. Read README.txt for controls, save location and recovery precautions.

Store purchases and refunds, if applicable to a future store edition, are handled under that store's process. This standalone Windows candidate includes all campaign content and has no in-game purchases.
