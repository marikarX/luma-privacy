Luma Android privacy policy

Synapse One · Luma: A Little Lightwork
Package: com.synapseone.luma
Effective October 18, 2026

Gameplay and local data

Luma saves completed chambers, Mastery awards, your resume chamber, sound preference, and any Open Lab setup you choose to save in the app's local storage. Local backups and diagnostic logs may also be created. The game does not upload your progress, optical arrangements, or gameplay events to a Synapse One server. It has no custom player accounts, advertising SDK, or analytics SDK.

Optional Google Play purchase

The opening 14 chambers are free. An optional one-time purchase unlocks the remaining campaign and all eight Open Lab experiments. Google Play handles checkout and payment details under Google's own privacy policy. Luma does not receive your payment-card details.

Luma uses Google Play Billing to retrieve product information, request purchases, check ownership, and acknowledge completed purchases. The app receives purchase information such as the product identifier, purchase token, order information when provided by Play, purchase state, and signed receipt. It verifies the receipt on your device and stores a signed receipt locally to support offline access. Purchase tokens are sent to Google Play when acknowledging or checking purchases; they are not sent to a Synapse One backend. A successful ownership check can remove a cached entitlement after a refund or other revocation.

Google Play and Android may process information under their own policies, including for payment, app delivery, security, and platform diagnostics. See Google's privacy policy. This policy does not control Google's processing.

The bundled Google Play Billing library also sends billing diagnostics to Google through its data transport component. This includes billing API results and errors, library and app versions, the app package, Android/device and network metadata, and a randomly generated billing-session identifier. This diagnostic processing supports operating and troubleshooting the store integration. It is separate from Luma's local gameplay saves and does not send your chamber progress or optical arrangements to Synapse One.

Google Play reviewers may enter a supplied review code to access paid content without making a purchase. The code is checked on the device and saved locally to preserve review access after restart. It is not sent to a Synapse One server and does not create a store purchase.

Permissions and connectivity

The game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location. Google Play connectivity is used for store features. Android accessibility integration exposes on-screen game descriptions and controls to an accessibility service you enable; it does not send them to Synapse One.

Removing local data

You can remove app-local saves, settings, and the cached receipt through Android Settings → Apps → Luma → Storage → Clear storage, or by uninstalling the app. Back up anything you want to keep first. Device backup and restoration may be managed separately by Android and your Google account. Clearing local data does not cancel a Play purchase or delete Google's transaction records. Restore purchase can retrieve an eligible purchase for the current Play account.

Support messages

If you email info@synapse-one.com, Synapse One receives your email address and the message or attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and investigate the issue. Do not send passwords or payment credentials. Request deletion of information you sent by contacting the same address and identifying your original request; transaction records held by Google must be managed with Google.

Children and changes

Luma does not ask players to provide personal information in the game. Store purchases are subject to Google Play account and parental controls. We will update this page if the app's data practices change.
